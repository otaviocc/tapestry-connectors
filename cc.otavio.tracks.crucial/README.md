This connector enables Tapestry to display public track shares from [Crucial Tracks](https://www.crucialtracks.org).

I, [Otávio](https://otavio.cc), am not affiliated with, nor do I work for, Crucial Tracks or its creators. This connector is independently developed as part of [my connectors open-source project](https://github.com/otaviocc/tapestry-connectors).
